1

What the platform has done so far

Where this comes from
The EduBrisk test exports for every grade, section and subject, downloaded from the platform, and the school's student login summary (number of students in each section).
What it shows
How much students have improved when they retook a test (first attempt against best attempt on the same test), how many students on the class list have taken at least one test, how many test attempts were made, and how many attempts were new tests versus repeats.
What it does not show
Hours on the platform for students (no student usage figures were supplied), or school exam marks. Improvement on retakes is within EduBrisk tests, not school exams.
Who is not in it
Students whose section is not on the login summary are left out of percentages (see the note below the participation table). Students who never opened a test do not appear in the exports, so they are counted only as "did not take".
2

Improvement on retaken tests

Where this comes from
Every test in the selection that a student attempted more than once. The platform records up to three attempts per test.
What it shows
The average mark on the first attempt (orange) and the average best mark (blue) across those retaken tests, for each group. The gap is how much students improved by retaking.
What it does not show
Whether students improved in school exams, or how students who did not retake would have done. Students who retake may be the ones most motivated to improve.
Who is not in it
Tests taken only once. Groups with fewer than three retaken tests are left out because one or two results move the average too much.

3

Who has taken tests

Where this comes from
Students on roll: the school's student login summary for 2026–27, which gives the number of students in each section. Students who took tests: the EduBrisk test exports.
What it shows
For each group, how many students took at least one test of the selected type, how many did not, and the share of the class list that took part. Tests sat counts each different test once per student; attempts include retakes.
What it does not show
Which students did not take a test: the summary gives counts, not names, so they cannot be listed. When a subject is chosen in Grades 11–12, every student in the section is still counted, though not all of them study that subject.
Who is not in it
Students who took tests under a section that is not on the login summary are shown separately and not counted in the percentages.
4

Subjects

Where this comes from
The EduBrisk test exports. Book titles are grouped under the subject they belong to (for example Poorvi, Kaveri and the grammar books under English; History, Geography, Political Science and Economics under Social Science up to Grade 10). Grades 11–12 keep their own subjects.
What it shows
For each subject, how many students took at least one test, how many different tests were sat, and how many attempts were retakes.
What it does not show
An average mark across chapters: each chapter reached a different set of students, so such an average would compare who sat what rather than how well. Choose a chapter to see marks test by test.
Who is not in it
Subjects and chapters that no student opened do not appear in the exports, so they are not listed.
5

This chapter, test by test

Where this comes from
The EduBrisk test export for the chosen subject, filtered to the chosen chapter.
What it shows
For each test in the chapter: how many students took it, the share of the class list, the average first-attempt and best marks, and how many students retook it.
What it does not show
The marks of students who did not take the test, or school exam marks for the chapter.
Who is not in it
Students whose section is not on the login summary are counted in the marks but not in the share of the class list.
6

Students who took tests

Where this comes from
The EduBrisk test exports. Names are as the students' platform accounts show them.
What it shows
Every student who took a test in the selection, with the number of tests, retakes, and time per test (the average time taken against the average time allowed). Click a student to open their full record.
What it does not show
Students who took no tests: the class list supplied gives counts only, not names.
Who is not in it
Students with no test in the selection.

Teachers on EduBrisk

Where this comes from
The school's teacher usage report for 2026–27: one row per teacher account, with the classes taught and hours on the platform.
What it shows
How many teacher accounts there are, how many have used the platform for at least an hour, the total hours, and how concentrated that use is.
What it does not show
When the hours were spent, sign-ins, or what was used. Hours are recorded in decimal hours (0.50 is 30 minutes).
Who is not in it
Accounts with no hours recorded in the usage report are shown as "not recorded", not as zero. Some teachers hold two accounts; each is listed separately.
